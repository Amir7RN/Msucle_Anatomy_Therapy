Which muscle is causing my hip pain?

Hip pain is frequently muscular — from the glutes that stabilise the pelvis to the deep rotators and hip flexors. Here are the muscles that most commonly cause hip pain and how to confirm yours on an interactive 3D model.

Muscles that commonly cause hip pain

  • Gluteus medius & minimus — Pain on the side of the hip, worse standing on one leg, walking, or lying on that side. A leading cause of lateral hip pain.
  • Piriformis — Deep buttock pain that can refer down the leg; tightens with prolonged sitting.
  • Tensor fasciae latae (TFL) — Front-outer hip tightness that feeds the IT band and can pinch at the hip.
  • Iliopsoas (hip flexor) — Deep front-hip pain and a pinch when lifting the knee or after long sitting.
  • Adductors (groin) — Inner-thigh and groin pain that flares with cutting, squeezing, or side movements.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find which hip muscle is causing pain?

Tap the sore spot on Zevahealth AI's 3D hip model. It highlights likely muscles like the gluteus medius or piriformis and helps you confirm the source, then offers relief exercises.

Why does the side of my hip hurt when I walk or lie on it?

Lateral hip pain with walking or side-lying often points to the gluteus medius/minimus. Zevahealth AI helps you confirm and target it with specific strengthening.

When is hip pain serious?

Seek care for hip pain after a fall, inability to bear weight, deformity, fever, or night pain that keeps worsening. Zevahealth AI is not a substitute for medical care.
